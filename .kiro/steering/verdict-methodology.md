# Steering — Verdict Methodology (evidence rules)

These rules govern all code generated for VerdictKit's apps. They encode the
evidence-checking discipline this skeleton exists to enforce:

1. **No firm verdict without two independent sources.** Two items from the
   same source are one source. If the threshold is not met, downgrade the
   verdict and say exactly what is missing.
2. **Excerpts are verbatim.** Never paraphrase, trim mid-sentence, or
   "clean up" a quoted excerpt inside the verdict path.
3. **Freshness is always shown.** Every citation carries its publication
   time; stale evidence decays by half-life and the decay is visible in the
   contribution score.
4. **Authority is explicit.** Official/first-hand = 3, mainstream
   second-hand = 2, everything else = 1. Never inflate.
5. **"Not found" is a result.** Missing evidence produces a downgrade with a
   named gap — never a guess dressed as a verdict.
6. **Refutation bars are asymmetric where a false kill is costly**
   (see ClaimCheck B3): killing a true claim needs stronger evidence than
   confirming one.
